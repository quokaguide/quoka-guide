Our Story

Sunset Bistro is a neighborhood spot built around simple goals: serve great food, treat people like family, and keep it local. Our menu changes with the seasons and features ingredients sourced from Florida farms whenever possible.

Whether you're grabbing a quick lunch or celebrating a big night out, we’ll make it easy (and delicious).
