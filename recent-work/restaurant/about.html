Our Story

A neighborhood restaurant focused on fresh ingredients and warm hospitality.

Built Around Local

Sunset Bistro started as a simple idea: take local ingredients, cook them with care, and serve them in a space that feels like home. We partner with Florida growers and suppliers whenever we can, and we update our menu with the seasons.

From weeknight dinners to celebrations, our team is here to make your visit easy and memorable.
